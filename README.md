# Comprehensive Customer Churn Analysis 

## Key Insights 
1. **High Customer Churn Rate**: Understand the factors leading to a 25% churn rate in the last quarter.
2. **Customer Engagement**: Customers with less than 2 interactions per month have a 40% higher risk of churning.
3. **Demographic Patterns**: Younger customers (18-25) show a 17% higher cancellation rate compared to older demographics.
4. **Feedback Loop**: 60% of churned customers reported dissatisfaction over missed promotional emails.
5. **Contract Duration Impact**: Contracts longer than 12 months had a 15% lower churn rate.
6. **Social Media Influence**: Positive feedback on social media reduces churn risk by 30%.

## Risk Matrix 
| Risk Level | Description               | Action Required                       |
|------------|---------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| High       | Customers with 5+ failed interactions | Immediate engagement & outreach     |
| Medium     | Customers with declining usage | Monitor & strategize engagement     |
| Low        | New customers within 30 days | Nurture relationship & provide content |

## Strategic Recommendations by Priority Level 
1. ** High Priority**: Enhance Customer Support Services for rapid response.
2. ** Medium Priority**: Launch re-engagement email campaigns for at-risk customers.
3. ** Low Priority**: Improve onboarding process for new customers.
